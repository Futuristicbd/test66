# FuturisticBD — Personal Branding reel v4

`next-reel-v4.mp4` · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 60 fps · 25.0 s · scored, normalised to −14 LUFS · real FuturisticBD logo.

The style follows the LangEase launch film:
- a pale cool ground, calm type, and blue-gradient keywords,
- glass objects moved by a hand cursor,
- a camera flight through a tunnel of phones,
- a ribbon that becomes a progress bar,
- a tilted dashboard and a button click,
- a sparkle that draws the tagline and turns into the logo.

## Sound (`motion/sound4.py`, toolkit in `motion/sfxlib.py`)
- **Music:** a 120 BPM tech-launch bed in E (Emaj9 – C#m7 – Amaj9 – B).
  - A soft intro; the beat enters with the folder.
  - Full energy through the tunnel, with 16th-note plucks and rushing air.
  - A build under the progress bar (accelerating ticks, drums drop, a riser) that lands on "Live." with a pop, confetti sparkles, a chime and a crash.
  - A lighter half-time breakdown for the booking moment, then the beat returns for "Plan. Shoot. Grow."
  - It resolves on an E-major chord with the logo.
- **Effects** are locked to the picture: a whoosh on every word and move, a pop for the folder, and a click on every tap of the hand cursor.
