## Sound (`motion/sound3.py`)
- **Dark half:**
  - a D-minor drone and air pad,
  - a heartbeat pulse under the mirror,
  - clock-tick tension under the search,
  - mechanical typing and an enter key,
  - a deep boom and near-silence on "nothing.", then a glass shatter,
  - flutter whooshes as the competitor content floods past,
  - trailer braams on "Everywhere." and "isn't enough.",
  - a riser into the light burst (impact, shimmer, crash).
- **Light half:**
  - a 120 BPM track in D (D–A–Bm–G): kick, clap, hats, shaker, pumping sub bass, sidechained supersaw chords and a pluck arpeggio,
  - a panned whoosh on every whip,
  - a tick and a swoosh on each list item,
  - a crash on "Handled.",
  - a fill and riser into the logo, then the logo hit, a chime and a resolving chord,
  - a button click with a chime.
- **Mix:** a reverb bus, glue compression and a soft clip. Loudness is normalised to −14 LUFS with a −1.2 dBTP ceiling.
